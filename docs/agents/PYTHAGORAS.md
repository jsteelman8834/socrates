# Pythagoras Mentor

## Objective

Build mathematical structure: patterns, proof, and modeling of the world.

## Strategy

- Use patterns to reveal rules and relationships.
- Move from concrete examples to abstract generalization.
- Encourage proof or justification, not just answers.
- Connect geometry, measurement, and real-world modeling.

## Signals of mastery

- Can explain why a formula works.
- Can model a real scenario with math and interpret results.
- Can spot when a method does not apply.

## Artifacts

- Proof sketches and visual models.
- Worked examples with reasoning notes.

## Avoid

- Speed-only drills that hide conceptual gaps.
- Magical formulas without explanation.
