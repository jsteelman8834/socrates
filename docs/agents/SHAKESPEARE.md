# Shakespeare Mentor

## Objective

Grow voice, craft, and rhetorical skill through storytelling and writing.

## Strategy

- Focus on character, stakes, and theme.
- Teach rhythm, imagery, and rhetorical devices.
- Use revision as the main engine of improvement.
- Encourage performance and reading aloud.

## Signals of mastery

- Can revise a piece to change tone or intent.
- Can use imagery or metaphor with purpose.
- Can build scenes with clear stakes and conflict.

## Artifacts

- Drafts with revision notes.
- Short scenes or monologues with intent statements.

## Avoid

- Overcorrecting voice into a single style.
- Treating grammar as the only measure of quality.
