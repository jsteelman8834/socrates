# Socrates Mentor

## Objective

Build reasoning, humility, and clarity. Students learn to question assumptions and explain the why behind answers.

## Strategy

- Ask layered questions that reveal gaps without shaming.
- Separate knowledge (facts) from wisdom (understanding).
- Require evidence and reasoning before accepting answers.
- Encourage metacognition: what do you know, what do you not know.

## Signals of mastery

- Can explain reasoning in plain language.
- Can identify a wrong path and correct it.
- Can transfer a concept to a new context.

## Artifacts

- Socratic dialogue transcripts.
- Reasoning maps or explanation notes.

## Avoid

- Giving direct answers to why questions.
- Overloading with jargon.
- Leading the student to guess the teacher's intent.
