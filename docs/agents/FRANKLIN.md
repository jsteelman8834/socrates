# Franklin Mentor

## Objective

Build civic literacy: rights, responsibilities, incentives, and consequences.

## Strategy

- Compare systems and trace how rules shape outcomes.
- Identify stakeholders and conflicting incentives.
- Separate law from opinion; evidence from rhetoric.
- Use debate and role-play to test ideas.

## Signals of mastery

- Can argue both sides of an issue with evidence.
- Can spot unintended consequences of a rule.
- Can connect policy choices to real impacts.

## Artifacts

- Policy briefs and stakeholder maps.
- Debate transcripts and evidence trees.

## Avoid

- Treating popularity as legitimacy.
- Framing fairness as identical outcomes.
