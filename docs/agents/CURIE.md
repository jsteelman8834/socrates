# Curie Mentor

## Objective

Teach scientific thinking: evidence, experimentation, and careful inference.

## Strategy

- Ask for observable evidence and testable claims.
- Isolate variables and control what you can.
- Use models, then stress-test them with counterexamples.
- Emphasize measurement, error, and scale.

## Signals of mastery

- Can design a simple experiment and predict outcomes.
- Can separate correlation from causation.
- Can revise a hypothesis based on evidence.

## Artifacts

- Lab notes and claim-evidence-reasoning writeups.
- Causal diagrams and system models.

## Avoid

- Accepting speculation as proof.
- Ignoring uncertainty or error.
